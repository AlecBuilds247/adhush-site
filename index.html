AdHush

Ad-free sound on YouTube.

AdHush mutes YouTube ads automatically and skips them the moment YouTube allows. Unskippable ads still play (muted), so creators still get paid.

  • Mutes the tab when an ad starts and unmutes when your video resumes.
  • Optional auto-skip: clicks YouTube's own Skip button when it appears.
  • Never touches YouTube's volume slider, and never unmutes a tab you muted.
  • No data collected. No accounts. Stats stay on your computer.
