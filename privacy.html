AdHush

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 14, 2026 · Applies to: AdHush browser extension, free version 1.x

Short version: AdHush collects no data. Nothing leaves your computer. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no third parties.

What AdHush does

AdHush is a Chrome extension that mutes your browser tab while a video ad plays on YouTube (www.youtube.com) and unmutes the tab when your video resumes.

Data we collect

None. AdHush does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, watch history, video titles, website content, or usage data. AdHush makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics or tracking code.

What is stored on your computer

AdHush saves a few values using Chrome's built-in extension storage (chrome.storage), which stays on your device:

Stored valueWhy
On/off settingRemember your choices
Counters: ads muted (today and total), minutes muted, today's dateShow your stats in the AdHush popup
Temporary per-tab ad state (cleared when the browser closes)Know which tabs AdHush muted, so it only unmutes those

These values are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling AdHush deletes them.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Access to www.youtube.com: to detect when YouTube's player is showing an ad. AdHush does not read or record what you watch.
  • Storage: to save your settings and counters on your computer (see above).
  • Tab muting: AdHush mutes and unmutes only the YouTube tab that is playing an ad. It never unmutes a tab you muted yourself.

What AdHush does not do

  • It does not block, hide, speed up or remove ads, and does not block any network requests.
  • It does not run on any website other than www.youtube.com.
  • It does not use cookies, fingerprinting or remote code.

Children

AdHush collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that need data (for example an optional paid upgrade), this policy will be updated before that version is released, with the effective date changed above, and the Chrome Web Store disclosures updated to match.
